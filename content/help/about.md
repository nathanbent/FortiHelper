## Use it for
Checking which version of FortiHelper you are looking at and what changed recently, for example after a new tool or option appears.

## You get
The version, the commit it was built from and the build date, then the changelog with the newest entries first. Older entries are folded under Earlier changes.

## Limits
The build details describe this copy of the site. A copy hosted elsewhere, or a local build, can be older or newer than the public one.

## More
The changelog is a file in the repository, and the source is linked from [the README]({repo}#readme).
