## Use it for
Building a whole config from one form: a new site, a VPN, a maintenance change. For a single kind of object from a list, use the generators such as [Object Helper](#/object-helper).

## Give it
Pick a template, then fill in its fields. Fill example values shows what a finished form looks like. To use your own template, open Use a different template and paste it or load the file.

## You get
The template's config with every `<placeholder>` replaced, to copy or download. A field left blank stays as its placeholder in the output, so it is easy to find.

## Limits
The built-in templates are generic examples, not a hardened baseline: read the result against your own standards and FortiOS version before using it.

A template you load is saved in this browser's local storage, not uploaded, until you choose Forget saved template. Do not save one on a shared computer if it holds site details.
